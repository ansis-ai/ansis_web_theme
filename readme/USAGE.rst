1. **Home Menu & Dashboard**:
   - Click the brand icon or hover over the top-left app title to click the ``<`` back chevron to open the Home Menu overlay.
   - Start typing immediately to filter apps in real-time.
   - Drag and drop app tiles to customize your dashboard layout.
   - Press ``ESC`` to close the overlay or reset search.

2. **Data & List Views**:
   - Table headers (``thead``) stay pinned to the top during vertical scrolling.
   - Selection checkboxes stay frozen on the left during horizontal scrolling.
   - Aggregate totals (``tfoot``) stay pinned to the bottom.

3. **Form Views & Mobile**:
   - Notebook tabs feature clean flat underline accents and badge pills.
   - Metric stat buttons on the top-right feature card elevation and hover lift animations.
   - On mobile devices (screen width < 768px), open the slide-out burger drawer with user profile header and 1-tap "All Apps" button.
